🛡️ TrustGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

✓ Privacy First

1. Overview

TrustGuard ("the App") is developed by TrustEdge Labs. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use our services.

TL;DR: TrustGuard does not collect personal data. All core features operate locally on your device. We use Firebase Analytics solely to collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

TrustGuard does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Contacts, messages, or call logs
  • Photos, videos, or files (recovered files stay on your device)
  • Browsing history or DNS query logs

3. How the App Works

All of TrustGuard's features operate entirely on your device:

  • Permission Analysis: Reads installed app information using Android's PackageManager API. This data is processed locally and never transmitted.
  • Ad & Tracker Blocking: Uses a local VPN service to filter DNS requests on your device. No DNS queries are sent to external servers for filtering purposes.
  • File Recovery: Scans your device's local storage for recoverable files. Recovered files are saved to your device's storage. No file data leaves your device.
  • Adware Detection: Analyzes installed apps' permissions and metadata locally. No data is sent externally.
  • App Activity Monitoring: Uses Android's UsageStatsManager to display app activity data that is already available on your device.

4. Analytics Data

TrustGuard uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics for app improvement purposes. The following anonymous data may be collected:

  • App usage statistics (screen views, session duration)
  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • Approximate location (country/region level, derived from IP address)
  • App performance data

This data is collected anonymously, is not used to identify you, and is used solely for app development purposes.

5. Internet Usage

TrustGuard uses internet access for:

  • Google Play Billing: To manage premium subscriptions through Google Play Store's official billing system. This connection is between your device and Google's servers only.
  • DNS Resolution: The local VPN service resolves DNS queries using Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) or Google (8.8.8.8) DNS servers for legitimate domain resolution.
  • Firebase Analytics: To send anonymous usage statistics to Google's servers via encrypted connection (HTTPS).

No personal data, app scan results, or device information is transmitted to TrustEdge Labs servers or any third party.

6. Permissions Explained

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Required to scan and analyze all installed apps on your device.
  • INTERNET: Required for Google Play subscription management only.
  • VPN Service: Required to create a local VPN tunnel for DNS-based ad and tracker blocking. No data is routed through external servers.
  • MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Required to scan for and recover deleted files from your device's storage.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS: Required to display app activity data (foreground/background time, network usage).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to show VPN protection status notifications.

7. Third-Party Services

The third-party services used are:

  • Google Play Billing Library: For processing premium subscription purchases. Google's privacy policy applies to billing transactions: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Analytics: For collecting anonymous app usage statistics. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

TrustGuard does NOT use any advertising SDKs, crash reporting services, or social media SDKs.

8. Data Storage

TrustGuard stores minimal preference data locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences:

  • Language preference
  • Premium subscription status
  • Free recovery count
  • VPN protection toggle state

This data is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

9. Children's Privacy

TrustGuard does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is safe for users of all ages.
